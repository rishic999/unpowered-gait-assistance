# Unpowered Gait Assistance

A mechanical device designed to assist walking without an external power supply. This work grew out of my research internship at **IIT Bombay**, where I co-invented the mechanism with the research team.

## The problem

Walking depends on coordinated movement of the foot and ankle. For people with weakened muscles, including some people recovering from a stroke, lifting the heel during walking can be difficult. The project explored a mechanical way to provide assistance at that point in the gait cycle.

## How the mechanism works

The design combines a torsion spring, a shoe-mounted channel, and a calf-mounted locking mechanism. According to the patent specification:

1. **Engage:** Movement between the heel and calf tightens a cord, engaging the lock.
2. **Store energy:** As the shin moves forward over the planted foot, the locked torsion spring bends and stores energy.
3. **Release:** At heel-off, the cord slackens and the mechanism unlocks, allowing the spring to release its stored energy.
4. **Reset:** The mechanism permits relative motion during the swing phase before the cycle repeats.

The design uses the geometry of walking to control the mechanism, without an external power supply or electrically powered sensors.
